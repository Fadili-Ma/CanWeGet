Privacy Policy

Effective: February 1, 2026

CanWeGet.com ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle data when you use our group buying platform. We prioritize transparency and minimal collection.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 No Personal Data Collection

CanWeGet.com is a frontend-only static website. We do not collect, store, or transmit personal information, emails, names, or identifiers through normal usage.

1.2 Local Storage (Browser-Only)

We use localStorage in your browser to persist:

  • Group deal participation counts
  • Created deals (local only)
  • Theme preferences (dark/light mode)
  • Temporary form data

This data never leaves your device. Clearing browser data removes it permanently. No server storage, no cross-device sync.

1.3 Contact Form Data

Messages sent via our contact form are processed by Formspree.io (third-party service). We receive submitted email, name, subject, and message. Formspree may retain data per their privacy policy.

1.4 Google AdSense

Ad Disclosure: This site uses Google AdSense. Google collects browsing data for personalized advertising. See Google's Privacy Policy.

2. How We Use Information

  • Local storage: Enable platform functionality (deal tracking, preferences)
  • Contact forms: Respond to inquiries (deleted after resolution)
  • Analytics: None (no tracking pixels or services)
  • Advertising: AdSense serves contextual ads based on page content and your browsing

We never sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties.

3. Cookies & Tracking

No cookies set by CanWeGet.com. Standard session cookies may be used by:

  • Google AdSense (advertising)
  • Formspree (contact forms)
  • Your browser (localStorage functionality)

Browser settings control cookie acceptance. Opt out of AdSense personalization via Google Ads Settings.

4. GDPR & Your Rights (EU/UK)

As a minimal-data platform, GDPR compliance is straightforward:

  • Right to Access: No personal data stored
  • Right to Deletion: Clear browser data
  • Right to Object: Disable cookies in browser
  • Data Portability: Not applicable

Contact us at contact@canweget.com for assistance.

5. Children's Privacy

CanWeGet.com does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Group buying coordination requires adult participation.

6. Third-Party Links & Services

  • Google AdSense: Advertising (see disclosure above)
  • Formspree.io: Contact form processing
  • Social media links: External platforms (Facebook, X, Instagram)

We have no control over third-party privacy practices. Review their policies separately.

7. Data Security

No server data storage = minimal attack surface. Local storage protected by browser security. Contact form data transmitted via HTTPS to Formspree.

We implement reasonable security for our static hosting, but cannot guarantee absolute security of internet transmission.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Significant changes posted on homepage and updated effective date above. Continued use constitutes acceptance.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this privacy policy? Email contact@canweget.com.
