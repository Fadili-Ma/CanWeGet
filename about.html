What is Group Buying?

Group buying turns individual demand into collective purchasing power. Here's how it works in practice.

At its core, group buying leverages a simple economic principle: suppliers offer better prices for larger orders. Individual consumers typically pay retail premiums for small quantities and frequent deliveries, absorbing margins that businesses avoid through volume purchasing.

CanWeGet.com bridges that gap. Whether you're coordinating with neighbours for monthly groceries, organizing office supplies with coworkers, or gathering friends for tech gadgets, our platform makes it easy to pool demand and unlock wholesale-level pricing.

The process is straightforward:

  • Create or join a deal with a clear target number of participants
  • Track real-time progress as others join
  • Once the threshold is met, everyone gets the group price
  • If the target isn't reached, no one pays—no risk, no hassle

This model works because it creates win-win outcomes: buyers get better prices, suppliers move more volume with lower acquisition costs, and everyone benefits from predictable savings.

Why Group Buying Saves You Money

The mathematics of volume purchasing create consistent, predictable savings on everyday expenses.

Retailers build pricing around economies of scale. A household spending $200 monthly on staples like cleaning supplies, toiletries, and paper goods pays full retail margins. Through group buying, that same basket drops 15-30% because suppliers pass along bulk discounts.

Consider office chairs: individual purchase might cost $150 each. A group of 10 buys at $110 per unit—a 27% savings that scales with larger groups. The platform handles coordination so you focus on the savings.

Annual impact compounds quickly. A family saving 20% on $2,400 yearly household goods nets $480 back in their pocket. Small businesses achieve enterprise-level pricing without enterprise budgets. Communities build sustainable cost-reduction systems for recurring needs.

Unlike flash sales or coupons, group buying creates structural savings through demand aggregation. It works for groceries, tech, fitness memberships, local services—any purchase where volume reduces per-unit costs.

How CanWeGet Works

Our platform streamlines the entire group buying process without complexity or hidden fees.

Anyone can create a deal by specifying the product, target participants, and deadline. Share the link with your network—friends, family, colleagues, neighbours. As people join, everyone sees real-time progress toward activation.

Smart features handle the details: automatic countdown timers, participant tracking, threshold validation, and deal status updates. No manual spreadsheets or chasing commitments—everything happens transparently on the platform.

We support both public deals (anyone can join) and private groups (invite-only). Creators set the rules: minimum group size, expected pricing, merchant details. The system ensures fair participation and clear expectations.

Payment only processes after successful group formation, eliminating risk. Our local storage keeps your deals and participation persistent across sessions, so you never lose progress.
